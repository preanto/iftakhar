--- layout: home author_profile: true classes: wide --- I am Iftakhar Ahmad, a science educator and research assistant with an interdisciplinary background in Zoology, Fisheries, and Education. My research interests include neuroscience, neuroimmunology, microbiology, and network pharmacology, with a particular focus on understanding neurological disorders and exploring innovative, evidence-based therapeutic approaches. Alongside my research activities, I have been working as a science teacher and laboratory demonstrator since 2016, combining academic knowledge with practical science education. I am also the founder of NeuroBridge Research Bangladesh, an initiative dedicated to advancing scientific research, collaboration, and knowledge sharing. Through research, teaching, and scientific innovation, I aspire to bridge the gap between biological science and human health, contribute to meaningful scientific discoveries, and inspire the next generation of researchers and learners.
